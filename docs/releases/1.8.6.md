## Canvas Loom 1.8.6

- 新增：可以从预览工作台导出完整的卡片预览图片。
- Added: Export complete card previews from the preview workbench.

- 新增：可以从单张文本卡片的右键菜单直接导出图片，并选择仓库内的保存文件夹。
- Added: Export an individual text card as an image from its context menu and choose a vault folder for saving it.

- 优化：导出图片时会保留完整的预览内容，不再截断滚动区域或把滚动条写入图片。
- Improved: Image exports preserve the complete preview without truncating scrollable content or including scrollbars.

- 修复：修正单张卡片导出无响应和导出空白图片的问题。
- Fixed: Fixed unresponsive single-card exports and blank exported images.

- 优化：改进 Canvas 缩放、性能模式和拼合后的卡片高度适配。
- Improved: Improved Canvas zoom handling, performance mode detection, and card height fitting after merging.

- 兼容性：完善旧版 Obsidian 设置界面的兼容提示与回退支持。
- Compatibility: Improved compatibility notices and fallback support for the legacy Obsidian settings view.
